Stems is a native macOS stem splitter for musician, producers, and DJs.

Download it from [GitHub](https://github.com/andreeeeaaaaas/stems-releases/releases)

## Problem
I always wondered how producers found high-quality samples from other songs, with  clean acapellas, drums, or instrumentals. The answer is typically uploading a song to a web service, with accounts, paywalls and queues or creative EQing, which can take hours and hours to refine.

After a bit of research into what was possible, I made Stems – a small, fast, dedicated Mac app with nothing else to install. Enjoy and let your 90's RNB remix dreams come true.

## Usage
1. Drop in a track or audio snippet (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC or AIFF)
2. Pick your settings (you can use your keyboard to navigate)
3. Wait and watch the stems grow
4. Preview the stems, delete the ones you don't want, and show the folder Finder

## Options

**Stems**: sets how many stems will be extracted from the audio.
- **2 stems**: vocal + instrumental
- **4 stems**: vocal, bass, drums, other
- **6 stems**: vocal, bass, guitar, piano, drums, other

**Processing**: chooses which model runs the process
- **Fast**: runs a smaller, faster job
- **HQ**: runs at the highest quality with minimal artefacts
