**"Som plommen i egget"**<br>Norwegian for 'Like the yolk in the egg'.

 **"No dar papaya"**<br>
 Spanish for 'don't give papaya'. Don't give reason for misfortune.

 **"The biggest problem with communication is the illusion that it has occurred"<br>**
 George Bernard Shaw.

 **"You can use an eraser on the drafting table, or a sledgehammer on the construction site"<br>**
 Frank Lloyd Wright.

 **"The future never arrives all at once"<br>**
 Neil Gaiman.

 **"L'appétit vient en mangeant"<br>**
 French for 'appetite comes with eating'.

 **"Specialisation is for insects"<br>**
 Robert A. Heinlein.

 **"A long muscle is a strong muscle"<br>**
 African proverb.

 **"Fasouli, to fasouli, yemizi to sakouli"<br>**
 Greek for 'bean by bean, you fill the sack'.

 **"If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together"<br>**
 African proverb.

 **"Sometimes it's better to travel than to arrive"<br>**
 Robert M. Pirsig, Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance.
